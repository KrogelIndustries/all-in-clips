All-In Clips

Long-form podcast episodes, cut into short-form clips and published to the accounts we own.

All-In Clips is a private, owner-operated tool. It turns full-length All-In Podcast episodes into short vertical clips — captioned, reframed, and cover-carded — and publishes them to social media accounts that we own and control on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Threads, using each platform's official developer API.

There is nothing here to sign up for. All-In Clips is not a consumer product. It has no public users, no accounts to create, nothing to download, and nothing for sale. The software runs on our own computers and authenticates only to our own social accounts. This website exists to document what the software does and how it handles data.

What All-In Clips does

The All-In Podcast publishes long-form episodes. Short vertical video is how most people discover a show today, but hand-cutting a two-hour episode into clips for five platforms is slow and repetitive. All-In Clips automates that work for our own channels:

  • Selects the most shareable moments from a full episode using the transcript.
  • Edits each moment into a vertical clip — reframed to keep the speaker centered, burned-in captions, a title cover, and loudness-normalized audio.
  • Tailors the cut to each destination: length, aspect, captions and cadence are tuned per platform.
  • Publishes the finished clips to our own accounts, on a schedule, at the privacy level we choose.
  • Measures how our own posts performed, so the next batch is chosen better.

How it works

  1. Ingest & transcribe. A new long-form episode is imported and transcribed. Candidate moments are ranked by how self-contained and shareable they are.
  2. Edit to vertical. Each selected moment is reframed to a 9:16 vertical, captioned, given a cover image, and audio-leveled. A quality check rejects clips that came out wrong.
  3. Tailor per platform. The same source moment is cut differently for each platform — clip length, caption style, hashtags and posting time follow what has worked best on that specific platform.
  4. Authorize. The operator signs in to each platform once through its official OAuth screen and grants the app permission to post to that owner account only.
  5. Publish & learn. Clips upload to our own accounts at the chosen time and privacy level. The app then reads the performance of those same posts to inform future selections.

Data and privacy, in brief

Because the app posts only to accounts we own, it does not collect information from members of the public. Through each platform's official API it receives, for our own accounts only: a basic account identifier (such as an open_id and username), an access token used to publish, and performance statistics for content we posted. Tokens and statistics are stored on computers we control and are never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Who operates it

All-In Clips is built and operated by All-In Podcast LLC for its own social media channels. It is not offered to third parties and does not act on behalf of any other person or account.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use All-In Clips for my own accounts?

No. It is private, owner-run software with no public sign-up. This site documents it for transparency and for platform review.

Does it post to anyone else's account?

No. Each platform authorization grants access to a single owner account, and the app only ever posts to that account.

Does it collect data about the public?

No. It reads identifiers, tokens and post-performance stats for our own accounts only. See the Privacy Policy.

Whose content is in the clips?

Clips are made from All-In Podcast episodes and are attributed to the source. Rights questions can be sent to the contact below.
